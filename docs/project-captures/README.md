# Project screenshots

16 complete frame exports from the supplied Figma documents. PNG masters are kept here; optimized WebP copies live in `src/assets/images/case-studies/` and are used by the site. Frames are shown in full, with no cropping.

- `pos-*` (5): retail checkout, payment methods, printer recovery, dark theme and till reconciliation. Company and client identifiers have been replaced with generic demo labels before export.
- `payments-*` (3): overview, transactions and payment links. The company logo, avatar and identifying references have been removed or replaced before export.
- `poke-*` (5): exploration, collection, card detail, trade proposal and chat.
- `fynaq-*` (3): original brand and website explorations, including the Chinese website concept. These are design explorations, not captures of the current live site.

Professional projects are presented with generic names. Confidential source links are intentionally excluded from public content. Interface figures are not presented as validated business results.

Anonymization used temporary copies of the source frames. These copies were removed after export. Original design frames were not edited.
